Countdownz Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

Countdownz is a countdown app for recording dates that matter to you. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information Countdownz stores

Countdowns you create, including their names, dates, repeat settings, symbols, and colours, are stored on your device. The app and its home screen widget use a shared on-device container so the widget can show your countdowns.

Countdownz does not send this information to the developer or to a Countdownz server. The app does not include an account system, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Sharing and retention

The developer does not collect, receive, or share personal information through Countdownz. Your countdowns remain available on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Apple may handle app data according to the device settings and Apple services you choose to use.

Children

Countdownz does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when that happens.

Contact

For privacy questions or app support, contact the developer through GitHub.
